Yin · Yang

Walk through the Professor’s room at Baze University: a 7.70 × 7.80 m seminar room divided by an S-curve of 43 walnut slats into a quiet study (yin) and a soft lounge (yang).

You start at the entrance door, looking into the lounge. Use the buttons along the bottom for the key views. The gold Photo buttons take you to the spots where the design renders were taken and dissolve into the photoreal image, with a slider to compare it against the live model.

Computer: W A S D or arrows to move, drag to look, double-click the floor to walk there. Phone: drag the circle to move, drag anywhere else to look, double-tap to walk there.

Drag the gold circle to walk, drag anywhere else to look around, double-tap the floor to walk there. The gear button opens settings; turn the phone sideways for a wider view.
